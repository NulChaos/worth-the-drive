    The built-in list covers about 400 places across Las Vegas, Henderson, Summerlin and North Las Vegas, captured from Google Maps on Oct 5, 2026, with Claude's summaries of recent reviews (the one-line takes, “order” and “heads-up” notes). With a Google API key in Settings, Refresh and Search pull live ratings, hours, prices and newly opened places; those get a Live tag. Places Google reports as permanently closed are hidden. Trust-adjusted rating pulls places with few reviews toward the local average (4.4★). Drive-thru means reviews mention one; Drive-thru? means it's a chain that usually has one. Grab & go marks counter-service spots. Budget uses Google's per-person price range when it has one, otherwise its $–$$$$ level ($ ≈ $10–20, $$ ≈ $20–30, $$$ ≈ $30–50, $$$$ ≈ $50+); “est.” means Claude estimated it. Distances are straight-line × 1.25 as a rough driving estimate. Map data and tiles © OpenStreetMap contributors.
